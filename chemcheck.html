About Us

This website was created as our final project for Mathematics in the Modern World. We developed this project to help people who are interested in science, as well as those who want to explore the field of science, understand basic scientific concepts and the mathematical computations connected to them. Through the interactive tools provided by ChemCheck, users can learn and practice basic chemistry concepts in a simple and accessible way.
